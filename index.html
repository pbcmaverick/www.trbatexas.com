About us

We exist to serve the churches

Trinity River Baptist Association is a fellowship of autonomous churches in Liberty and the surrounding communities of Southeast Texas. Our churches cooperate voluntarily to advance the gospel, strengthen congregations, support pastors and carry out ministry together.

Our vision

To see healthy, gospel-centered churches working together to reach their communities, develop leaders, strengthen families, and multiply disciples and churches throughout Southeast Texas and beyond.

Our mission

To connect, equip and strengthen local churches by encouraging pastors, developing leaders, providing resources, fostering cooperation, and supporting church planting and revitalization for the advancement of the gospel.

What we do

Churches helping churches

Building real relationships among pastors, churches, leaders and ministry partners.

  • Pastor gatherings and fellowship

Providing training, resources, coaching and practical ministry support.

  • Support for small, rural and pastorless churches
  • Conferences that serve the whole family

What we believe

Our churches affirm the Baptist Faith and Message (1963 or 2000). Here is the heart of those convictions. Tap any one to read more and see the Scriptures behind it.

The BibleGod's inspired, true and trustworthy Word.

The Bible is God's revelation of Himself to us. Its human authors wrote as they were moved by the Holy Spirit, so every word of Scripture is truly God's Word, without any mixture of error.

Because it comes from God, the Bible is our final authority for what we believe and how we live. We preach it, teach it and test everything by it, and we read its whole story as pointing to Jesus Christ.

“All scripture is given by inspiration of God, and is profitable for doctrine, for reproof, for correction, for instruction in righteousness.”

2 Timothy 3:16

Also see: 2 Peter 1:20–21 · Psalm 119:105 · Hebrews 4:12 · John 5:39

GodOne God, eternally existing as Father, Son and Holy Spirit.

There is one living and true God. He is the Creator, Redeemer and Ruler of the universe, infinite in holiness, perfect in love and wisdom, and worthy of all our worship and obedience.

God has revealed Himself as Father, Son and Holy Spirit. These three are not three gods but one God, each fully divine and equal in nature and glory, working together in creation and in our salvation.

“Hear, O Israel: The LORD our God is one LORD.”

Deuteronomy 6:4

Also see: Genesis 1:1 · Isaiah 6:3 · Matthew 28:19 · 2 Corinthians 13:14

Jesus ChristGod the Son, who died for our sins and rose again.

Jesus Christ is the eternal Son of God. He was conceived by the Holy Spirit, born of the virgin Mary, and lived a fully human life without sin, perfectly obeying the Father.

On the cross He died in our place, bearing the penalty for our sin. He rose bodily from the dead on the third day, ascended to the Father, and now reigns as Lord. One day He will return in power and glory.

“And the Word was made flesh, and dwelt among us, … full of grace and truth.”

John 1:14

Also see: Luke 1:35 · Philippians 2:5–11 · 1 Corinthians 15:3–4 · Acts 1:11

The Holy SpiritGod's Spirit gives new life and lives in every believer.

The Holy Spirit is fully God. He convicts the world of sin, righteousness and judgment, and He draws people to Christ and gives them new birth.

At the moment of salvation the Spirit comes to live in every believer, sealing them as God's own. He guides us into truth, grows Christlike character in us, and gives every believer spiritual gifts to serve the church.

“But ye shall receive power, after that the Holy Ghost is come upon you: and ye shall be witnesses unto me.”

Acts 1:8

Also see: John 16:7–13 · Titus 3:5 · Ephesians 1:13–14 · 1 Corinthians 12:4–7

Humanity and sinEvery person is made in God's image, and all have fallen into sin.

God created man and woman in His own image as the crown of His creation. Because every person bears God's image, every human life has dignity and worth from conception to natural death.

In the beginning humanity was innocent, but by our own choice we sinned against God. Since then every person is born with a nature inclined toward sin and needs God's grace to be made right with Him.

“So God created man in his own image, in the image of God created he him; male and female created he them.”

Genesis 1:27

Also see: Genesis 3:1–7 · Psalm 139:13–16 · Romans 3:10–12 · Romans 5:12

SalvationGod's free gift, received by grace through faith in Christ alone.

Every person has sinned and is separated from God. We cannot earn our way back by good works, religion or effort.

Salvation is God's free gift through the death and resurrection of Jesus. We receive it by turning from our sin and trusting Christ as Lord and Savior. Those who are truly born again are kept secure by God's power and will never be lost.

“For by grace are ye saved through faith; and that not of yourselves: it is the gift of God: not of works, lest any man should boast.”

Ephesians 2:8–9

Also see: Romans 3:23 · Romans 6:23 · Romans 10:9–10 · John 10:28–29

The churchA local body of baptized believers, gathered to worship and make disciples.

A New Testament church is a local, autonomous congregation of baptized believers, joined by covenant in the faith and fellowship of the gospel. It worships together, observes the ordinances, and works to take the gospel to the ends of the earth.

Each church governs itself under the lordship of Christ. Its biblical officers are pastors and deacons. Both men and women are gifted for service in the church, and the office of lead pastor is limited to men as qualified by Scripture.

“And they continued stedfastly in the apostles' doctrine and fellowship, and in breaking of bread, and in prayers.”

Acts 2:42

Also see: Matthew 16:18 · Ephesians 4:11–12 · 1 Timothy 3:1–13 · Hebrews 10:24–25

Baptism and the Lord's SupperTwo ordinances Christ gave His church.

Baptism is the immersion of a believer in water in the name of the Father, the Son and the Holy Spirit. It pictures our faith in Christ's death, burial and resurrection, our death to sin and our new life in Him. It is a step of obedience that follows salvation.

The Lord's Supper is a memorial in which the church, through the bread and the cup, remembers Christ's death for us and looks forward to His return.

“Therefore we are buried with him by baptism into death: that like as Christ was raised up from the dead … even so we also should walk in newness of life.”

Romans 6:4

Also see: Matthew 28:19 · Acts 2:41 · 1 Corinthians 11:23–26

Evangelism and missionsEvery Christian and every church is sent to make disciples of all nations.

Jesus commanded His followers to make disciples of all nations. Sharing the gospel is the privilege and duty of every Christian and every church, by our words and by the way we live.

That is why our churches cooperate: we pray, give, go and send together, in our own communities, across North America and around the world, so that every person can hear about Jesus.

“Go ye therefore, and teach all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost.”

Matthew 28:19

Also see: Matthew 28:20 · Acts 1:8 · Romans 10:14–15 · 2 Corinthians 5:20

CooperationChurches working together to do more than any could alone.

Every local church is autonomous, yet no church was meant to serve alone. Churches may freely organize associations and conventions to carry out the work of God's kingdom more effectively, without any authority over one another.

That is why Trinity River Baptist Association exists: to help our churches pray, give, train, send and serve together, so that the gospel goes further from Southeast Texas than any one congregation could take it.

“For we are labourers together with God.”

1 Corinthians 3:9

Also see: Acts 15:1–35 · 2 Corinthians 8:1–7 · Philippians 1:3–5 · Ephesians 4:15–16

Marriage and familyMarriage is one man and one woman for life, and the family is God's design.

God created the family as the foundation of human society. Marriage is the uniting of one man and one woman in a lifelong covenant, a picture of Christ's love for His church.

Husbands and wives are of equal worth before God and are called to love, serve and honor one another. Children are a gift from the Lord, and parents are called to teach them, by word and example, to love and follow Him.

“Therefore shall a man leave his father and his mother, and shall cleave unto his wife: and they shall be one flesh.”

Genesis 2:24

Also see: Matthew 19:4–6 · Ephesians 5:21–33 · Psalm 127:3 · Deuteronomy 6:6–7

Last thingsJesus will return, raise the dead and judge the world in righteousness.

In His own time God will bring the world to its appropriate end. Jesus Christ will return personally and visibly in glory, the dead will be raised, and Christ will judge all people in righteousness.

Those who have not trusted Him will be separated from God forever in hell. The redeemed will receive resurrection bodies and live forever with the Lord in heaven, where He will make all things new.

“And if I go and prepare a place for you, I will come again, and receive you unto myself; that where I am, there ye may be also.”

John 14:3

Also see: Acts 1:11 · 1 Thessalonians 4:16–17 · Matthew 25:31–46 · Revelation 21:1–4

Church planting

Church Planting Pipeline

Being built with our churches

Local churches plant churches. The association helps them do it.

We're building a new church planting pipeline together with our member churches. TRBA won't plant churches apart from them. Our role is to be a catalyst, so that planting new churches becomes part of the mission our churches share.

The heart of the pipeline

A church planting residency

Instead of sending a future planter away for training, a TRBA church hosts him as a resident. He serves, learns and leads inside a real church, under its pastor, while he's assessed, trained and coached. When he launches, he goes out with a sending church behind him.

Jesus sent His church to make disciples of all nations (Matthew 28:19–20). Planting new churches is one of the clearest ways to do that, so we'll help any member church set up a church planting residency.

  1. 1

    Called

    A man with a possible call to plant is recognized, often through his church's DAVID process.

  2. 2

    Assessed

    He and his family go through church planter assessment with Send Network, alongside his pastor's discernment.

  3. 3

    Resident

    He serves in a sending church: preaching, leading, making disciples and learning to plant, with Pastor Essentials training, a mentor and regular coaching.

  4. 4

    Sent

    He launches with his sending church, partner churches from across TRBA, and Southern Baptist support.

  5. 5

    Multiplying

    Coaching continues after launch, and the new church becomes a sending church itself.

Want your church to become a multiplying church? TRBA will walk with you to host a resident, using a one-year plan that fits your church's size. The resident learns your church's ministries and then launches either a new, independent church or a campus of your church that becomes self-governing over time. Talk with us.

A new church sponsored by a TRBA church for at least a year can join the association without the watchcare year.

About God

God wants a personal relationship with you

Maybe you've never opened a Bible or set foot in a church. That's okay. Here, in plain words, is the good news the Bible tells: who God is, what went wrong between us and Him, what Jesus did about it, and how you can know Him personally.

1

God made you, and He loves you

The Bible is God's message to us. It begins by telling us that God made everything, including you. You are not an accident. God made you on purpose, and He made you to know Him.

God is not a distant force or a strict judge waiting for you to slip up. He is a loving Father who knows your name, sees your life and wants a real relationship with you, one that lasts forever.

“For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life.”John 3:16
2

Something has gone wrong: sin

If God loves us, why do we feel far from Him? The Bible's answer is sin. Sin isn't only big crimes. It is anything we think, say or do that goes against God's good ways: lying, selfishness, pride, hurting others, or simply living as if God doesn't matter.

Every one of us has sinned, and sin has a cost. It separates us from God, who is perfectly good, and its end is death. We can't fix it by being nicer, going to church or doing more good than bad. A debt this big needs someone else to pay it.

“For all have sinned, and come short of the glory of God.”Romans 3:23
3

Jesus came to bring us back

Jesus is God the Son, who became a man about two thousand years ago. He lived the perfect life we could never live. He never sinned once.

Then He did something astonishing. He willingly died on a cross and took the punishment our sins deserved, so that we could be forgiven. Three days later He rose from the dead, proving He has power over sin and death. Jesus is alive today, and He is the only way back to God.

“But God commendeth his love toward us, in that, while we were yet sinners, Christ died for us.”Romans 5:8
4

Turn to Jesus and trust Him

Forgiveness is a gift. You can't earn it or buy it; you can only receive it. The Bible describes receiving it in two ways.

Repent means to turn around. You agree with God that your sin is wrong, and you choose to turn from it toward Him. Believe means to trust, the way you trust a chair to hold you when you sit down. You stop relying on your own goodness and rely completely on Jesus to save you, and you follow Him as the Lord of your life.

“That if thou shalt confess with thy mouth the Lord Jesus, and shalt believe in thine heart that God hath raised him from the dead, thou shalt be saved.”Romans 10:9
5

Your new life begins

When you trust Jesus, God forgives every sin, past, present and future, and adopts you into His family. His Holy Spirit comes to live in you, helping you change from the inside out and giving you strength to follow Jesus.

You won't be perfect overnight, but you are no longer alone. You have a Father who will never leave you, a church family to walk with, and the promise of eternal life with God.

“Therefore if any man be in Christ, he is a new creature: old things are passed away; behold, all things are become new.”2 Corinthians 5:17

Talking to God

There are no magic words. You are saved by trusting Jesus, not by saying a prayer. But if you want a place to start, you can tell God something like this:

“Lord Jesus, I know I am a sinner and I cannot save myself. I believe You died on the cross for my sins and rose from the dead. I turn from my sin and put my trust in You as my Lord and Savior. Thank You for forgiving me and giving me eternal life. Help me follow You from this day on. Amen.”

Your next steps

A relationship with God is a new beginning, not a finish line. Here is how to start growing.

Know you are saved

Your salvation rests on God's promise, not on how you feel on a given day.

1 John 5:13
Be baptized

Baptism is a public step of obedience that shows you now belong to Jesus.

Acts 2:41
Find a church family

You were not meant to follow Jesus alone. A local church will help you grow.

Hebrews 10:24–25
Read your Bible

Start with the Gospel of John and read a little every day.

Psalm 119:105
Pray every day

Prayer is simply talking with God. Tell Him your thanks, your needs and your worries.

Philippians 4:6
Tell someone

Share what God has done with a friend, a family member or a pastor.

Romans 1:16

Questions people ask

Is it too late for me?

No one is beyond God's reach. The apostle Paul called himself the worst of sinners, and God saved him and used him to spread the gospel.

1 Timothy 1:15

Do I have to clean up my life first?

No. Christ died for us while we were still sinners. Come to Him as you are, and He will make you new.

Romans 5:8 · 2 Corinthians 5:17

What about all the good things I've done?

Good works can't erase sin or earn God's favor. Salvation is a gift, received by faith in what Jesus has already done.

Ephesians 2:8–9 · Titus 3:5

Can I really know I'm saved?

Yes. God wants you to be sure. Your eternal life is held secure by Jesus, not by your own strength.

1 John 5:13 · John 10:28

What if I still have doubts and questions?

Bring them. God is not afraid of honest questions, and one of our pastors would be glad to talk with you.

Mark 9:24

No church should do ministry alone

Being part of Trinity River Baptist Association isn't about buying services. It's about having a family of churches and an association staff that come alongside your pastor and your people: in the good seasons, the hard ones, and everything in between.

01

We know your pastor by name

Before there's ever a crisis, your pastor has someone who calls, visits, prays and asks how he and his family are really doing.

02

We show up

When a pastor resigns, a storm hits or a church hits a hard season, we're there to walk with you, not hand you a brochure.

03

We help you do what's hard alone

Training, conferences, coaching and church planting that a single congregation would struggle to provide on its own.

How it works

  1. 1

    Start a conversation

    Call or write us. We'd love to hear your church's story.

  2. 2

    Get to know each other

    We'll visit, meet your pastor and leaders, and answer your questions, including about our shared statement of faith, the Baptist Faith and Message (1963 or 2000).

  3. 3

    A year of watchcare

    If your church decides to apply, our Executive Board welcomes you into a year of “watchcare.” From the start, your church receives the same help and benefits as every member church: pastor care, training, conferences, coaching and more. You give alongside our churches and sit in on Board meetings. The only difference is that you don't vote yet.

  4. 4

    Welcome to the family

    After watchcare, your church sends a letter asking to send messengers, and the association welcomes you at our Annual Meeting.
